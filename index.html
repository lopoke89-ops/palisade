      HOW TO PLAY

      TOUCH

      Left thumb: move
      Right thumb: aim, push far to fire
      ROCKET / STEALTH: the soldier's and sniper's ability (co-op)
      BUILD: place, upgrade or repair the tile you face. DOOR lets you and Delgado walk through.

      KEYBOARD

      WASD move · mouse aim
      Hold click to fire (soldier: bursts) · Space build
      1 2 3 material · F wall/door
      G grenade · E armory at the stake
      Shift sprint (quartermaster) · Q rocket (soldier) or stealth (sniper), co-op · B build kit · Enter start raid · T chat online · Esc pause

      CONTROLLER

      Left stick move · right stick aim · RT fire
      LT grenade · A build (hold to keep building)
      B ability, or sprint if your class has no ability · X armory at the stake
      Y wall/door · d-pad left/right material · d-pad down build kit
      View start raid · Menu pause. Change any button in Settings.
