    Hold the stake through the raids. Short runs end at night; Endless keeps coming and cycles day to night. Dell gathers, repairs, restocks and picks you up when you drop. Drop raiders for salvage and spend it at the stake between raids.

    MULTIPLAYER

    Up to six players over the internet, on phones or computers. One person hosts and gets a 4-letter room code; everyone else types it in.

    Online play needs the hosted copy of the game (the web link, or the icon on your home screen). This preview can't reach the internet, so solo only here.
